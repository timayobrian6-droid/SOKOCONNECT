About SokoConnect

SokoConnect is a Kenyan agricultural marketplace that connects farmers, suppliers and buyers directly. We make it easy to discover farm-fresh produce, grains, livestock, dairy and farm supplies, compare prices and buy with confidence.

For buyers

Shop from verified sellers across the country, pay with M-Pesa or cash on delivery, and track every order from your account.

For farmers & suppliers

List your products for free, reach thousands of buyers and manage your listings and sales from a simple seller dashboard.
