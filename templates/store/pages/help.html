Tracking your order

Log in and open My orders to see the status of every order. Pending orders can be cancelled from the order page.

Delivery

We deliver countrywide. Delivery is free for orders over KSh 5,000; otherwise a flat fee of KSh 250 applies.

Payment options

Pay via M-Pesa (Paybill instructions are shown after checkout), cash on delivery, or card on delivery.

Returns & refunds

If a product arrives damaged or not as described, contact us within 24 hours and we'll arrange a replacement or refund.
